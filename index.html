📦 Package as Sideloadable APK

Run KOMOTE as a true standalone Android application

⚡ Method 1: Instant WebAPK (Recommended)

Google Chrome on Android can automatically compile KOMOTE into a real Android APK (WebAPK) signed on your device:

  1. Open KOMOTE in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap Chrome's 3-dot menu (⋮).
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Android generates an APK in your app drawer that runs in its own process, completely independent of the browser!
📱 Method 2: PWABuilder (Downloadable .apk File)

Microsoft's free open-source PWABuilder turns any hosted web app into an installable Android APK file:

  1. Go to www.pwabuilder.com.
  2. Paste your hosted KOMOTE URL (e.g. GitHub Pages).
  3. Click "Package for Android".
  4. Download the generated .apk file, transfer to your phone, and tap to install!
💡 Why the controller disconnected when screen turned off:
Android and iOS freeze all background event loops when the screen sleeps. We added "Keep Screen Awake" in KOMOTE Settings (enabled by default) so your phone screen stays active at minimum brightness on your table while you read on your Kindle!
